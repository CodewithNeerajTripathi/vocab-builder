Learn the Words

In this section, you will explore new words through carefully designed word sets. Each set helps you understand meanings, recognise usage, and build confidence step by step.

  • Go through each word in the set carefully.
  • Read the meaning and try to understand how the word is used.
  • Take your time! Learning words well is more important than learning fast.

What You Will Do

  • Learn 5 sets of 20 words (100 words in total).
  • Explore a mix of everyday, academic, and non-English words to broaden your understanding of language.
  • After each set, attempt Quiz to check your understanding.

How to Learn Better

  • Try to use each word in a sentence in your mind or notebook.
  • Look for these words when you are reading or listening.
  • Revise words regularly to remember them better.
  • Focus on understanding the meaning, not just memorising.

Audio Support

  • Use the audio feature (if available) to hear word pronunciations and meanings.
  • Listening can help improve both understanding and pronunciation.

By the end of this section, you will recognise new words and will be ready to use them confidently in communication!

Use the Words: Writing and Speaking

In this section, you will complete different writing activities using the interface provided. Each activity is designed to help you improve your writing skills, use vocabulary correctly, and express your ideas clearly.

How to Attempt the Activity

  • Carefully read the instructions given.
  • Plan your ideas before you start writing.
  • Organise your ideas logically.
  • Use the required target words if they are given in the activity.
  • Make sure your writing is clear and meaningful.

Tips for Better Writing

  • Think about what you learned.
  • Use your imagination to make your writing interesting.
  • Do not rush. Take your time to check your work before submitting.
  • Try to use new words in a natural way.
  • Sample answers are only there as cues. Do your best to come up with your own answer that is imaginative, creative and shows your abilities.
  • Cues to think and help your writing are included.
  • A checklist of Success Criteria is also given for self-assessment.

Challenge Yourself

  • Add more details to your writing.
  • Write more sentences than required.
  • Use additional vocabulary words that you know.
  • Make your story or response more creative and engaging.

Accessibility Feature

  • You can use the audio feature (if available) to listen to the instructions, sample answers, etc.
  • This can help you understand the task better and support your learning.

Saving Your Work

  • After completing your writing, you can download your response as a PDF.
  • Save the file on your computer or device for future reference.
  • You can also review or share your work later if needed.

Once you are done writing; record your response using the recording feature in the Speaking section. This helps you practise speaking clearly, confidently, and with expression.

When you record your answer, you learn to:

  • Express your ideas in your own words
  • Speak with clarity and confidence
  • Focus on pronunciation, tone and fluency

Record and Save Your Answer

You can also record your response using the recording feature. You will be able to:

  • Record your answer using your voice
  • Play back your recording to check it
  • Delete and re-record if you want to improve it
  • Download your recording and save it on your device

This is a great way to practise speaking and expressing your ideas clearly.
