Use the Words: Writing and Speaking

How to Attempt the Activity

  • Carefully read the instructions given.
  • Plan your ideas before you start writing.
  • Organise your ideas logically.
  • Use the required target words if they are given in the activity.
  • Make sure your writing is clear and meaningful.

Tips for Better Writing

  • Think about what happened, what you did, and what you learned.
  • Use your imagination to make your writing interesting.
  • Do not rush. Take your time to check your work before submitting.
🏅

Challenge Yourself

Once you finish the task, you can:

  • Add more details to your writing
  • Write more sentences than required
  • Use additional vocabulary words that you know
  • Make your story response more creative and engaging.
